Operational Guide

TYPEFORM transforms paragraphs, phrases, or repeated words into structured typographic box compositions shaped by an uploaded silhouette or reference figure.

01

Text Input & Distribution

Enter any source paragraph into the multiline editor. Live word and character counts update immediately. Enable Repeat Text to cycle through your paragraph until the silhouette is completely filled, or disable it to place each word once and stop when the text is exhausted.

Choose between Sequential reading order, Randomized word order controlled by a deterministic seed, or Custom Word / Phrase repetition.

02

Image Upload & Mask Extraction

Upload a PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WebP file via drag-and-drop or the file browser, or select one of the built-in procedural silhouettes. Transparent PNGs can extract their silhouette directly from the alpha channel.

For opaque photographs and graphics, adjust Threshold, Contrast, Foreground Mode, and Invert Mask while observing the binary mask preview or the interactive Mask Overlay and Before / After split view.

03

Typography & Box Geometry

Configure font family, size, weight, style, case transformation, letter tracking, and internal box alignment. Every word is measured prior to placement so rectangles adapt cleanly without unintended clipping.

Select from five layout engines: Word Boxes (rectangles sized to each measured word), Variable-Width Rows, Fixed Uniform Grid, Dense Typography, or Loose Typography.

04

Palette & Multi-Resolution Export

While the studio interface remains strictly monochromatic, artwork colors can be configured using Monochrome, Two-Color, Random Palette, Custom Sequence, Grayscale, or Gradient modes with per-swatch frequency weights.

Click Export to download vector SVG (with optional glyph-to-path outline conversion), lossless PNG (with optional alpha transparency), or print-ready JPG at native 1×, 2×, 3×, or 4× resolution multipliers.
